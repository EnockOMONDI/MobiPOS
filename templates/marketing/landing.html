Problems we solve

Where traditional POS systems stop, losses begin.

Most growing mobile businesses do not lose money because they cannot sell. They lose money because devices move faster than their controls, records are incomplete, and no one can answer who handled a phone at each stage.

Lost devices

IMEIs disappear when purchasing, transfers, agent allocation and sales are tracked in separate places.

Stock discrepancies

Branches, warehouses and field teams show different numbers because movement records are delayed or manual.

No transfer accountability

Businesses struggle to prove who dispatched, received, rejected or adjusted stock.

Duplicate IMEI entry

Staff waste time typing identifiers again during transfers, allocations, sales and returns.

Weak agent visibility

Owners cannot easily see which agent or DSA holds which device and what happened next.

Slow stock takes

Manual counting and spreadsheet uploads delay receiving and create preventable errors.

Thin reporting

Sales totals alone do not explain aging stock, unpaid credit, commissions, repairs or suspicious activity.

Hard scaling

More branches, agents and stock create operational pressure when controls are not centralized.
