Status notes

What works today and what needs provider setup.

Production boundary
Email/process documentation: MobiPOS currently documents operational notifications and approval flows. Production email branding and delivery policy should be finalized per tenant.
Payment documentation: Cash, card, bank, credit and M-Pesa references are supported at workflow level. Live M-Pesa Daraja confirmation and reconciliation remain pending.
Tax process documentation: eTIMS has an integration event foundation, but live KRA provider submission is not activated.
Offline process documentation: offline invoice queue is treated as a local draft queue that syncs sanitized data. Final production policy must decide whether true offline sales are allowed.
Monitoring and backup documentation: persistent media storage, Sentry, automated backup/restore and Render browser smoke tests remain production-readiness work.
