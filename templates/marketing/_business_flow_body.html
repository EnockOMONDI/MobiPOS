Current status

What is ready now and what needs final setup.

Setup notes
Notifications and approvals are part of the workflow. Final email branding and delivery rules should be set for each client before launch.
Cash, card, bank, credit and M-Pesa reference recording are available in the workflow. Live M-Pesa confirmation and automatic reconciliation still need provider setup.
eTIMS is planned for tax compliance, but live submission to the tax provider is not active yet.
Offline invoices should be handled carefully. The recommended approach is to keep them as controlled drafts until the system can confirm and sync them safely.
Before a full production rollout, the client should confirm media storage, backups, monitoring, error reporting and recovery procedures.
